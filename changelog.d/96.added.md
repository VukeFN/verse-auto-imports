**Auto-import scope**: a new `verseAutoImports.general.autoImportScope` setting bounds which documents automatic imports may edit. `allFiles` (the default) keeps the current behavior, `openFiles` restricts edits to documents you have open, and `activeFile` restricts them to the editor you are working in. Previously the Verse compiler's project-wide diagnostics could make the extension open and edit files you had never opened. Quick fixes and CodeLens are unaffected in every scope.
